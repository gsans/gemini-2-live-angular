# Whiteboard Infographic Design Patterns

## 1. Sequential Process (The "Journey")
- **Structure**: 3-7 steps connected by winding arrows.
- **Visuals**: Steps are usually arranged in a "Z" or "S" curve across the board.
- **Use Case**: Explaining a workflow, a roadmap, or a step-by-step guide.
- **Prompt Addition**: "Arrange the steps in a winding S-curve across the whiteboard."

## 2. Comparison (The "Before vs. After")
- **Structure**: Two main boxes side-by-side.
- **Visuals**: A vertical line down the middle of the board. Left side is "Old Way" (maybe in red/black), right side is "New Way" (in blue/black).
- **Use Case**: Highlighting the benefits of a solution or comparing two concepts.
- **Prompt Addition**: "A side-by-side comparison with a vertical divider. Left side labeled 'OLD WAY' with messy scribbles, right side labeled 'NEW WAY' with clean, organized boxes."

## 3. Framework (The "2x2 Matrix")
- **Structure**: Four quadrants defined by two intersecting axes.
- **Visuals**: Hand-drawn axes with labels at the ends. Each quadrant contains a box with a title and icon.
- **Use Case**: Categorizing items or showing relationships between two variables.
- **Prompt Addition**: "A hand-drawn 2x2 matrix with labeled axes. Each quadrant contains a sketchy box with a title and a simple icon."

## 4. Central Concept (The "Mind Map")
- **Structure**: One central box with several branches radiating outwards.
- **Visuals**: A large central box for the main topic, with smaller boxes connected by thin, wavy lines.
- **Use Case**: Breaking down a complex topic into its core components.
- **Prompt Addition**: "A central main box with 4-6 branches radiating outwards to smaller sketchy boxes. Each branch is a wavy hand-drawn line."
